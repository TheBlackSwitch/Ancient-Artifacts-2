Day 0: 
Today was my lucky day. I started my day with falling into a big cave and finaly finding enough diamonds to craft full netherite armor. When I finished enchanting everything, an artifact smith nocked onto my door, telling me he had found a mysterious exploration map. I got very excited and instantly bought the map. The rest of the day was spent prepairing everything I needed for my adventure. 

Day I: 
This is the start of my new adventure. I grabbed all my potions and artifacts, put on my freshly enchanted armor and of I went! I said goodbye to everyone in the village and started following the map. On the way there I found a new artifact! Beefy Crystal. And 3 new artifact upgrades! 

Day II: 
I looked at my map and saw that I'm almost there. Woah there it is, a giant temple with an even bigger statue ontop. I entered the temple and "Oh mighty jesus" there's a lot of mobs inside there! Phew.. "This time I'll be smart", I whispered to myself and smart I was. I quickly activated reaching bracelet and broke all spawners, then I grabbed my sword and killed all mobs with ease! 

Day III (i guess):
Now it's time for the real adventure. I went downstairs and found out these dam stairs are very long! When I felt like I was almost there, I quickly made a little spawn room with extra weapons in case of emergency, a bed if I'm tired and some food and potions to regen my health. 

Day V (or so):
I'm really starting to lose count of my days. I picked final shout crown, upgraded my beefy crystal and went down into the depths. When I was fully downstairs, a lightning strike flashed in the room next to me. Another one and another one until a big living block of stone with creepy eyes came out of the ground. It came after me! I ran for my life and took hit after hit. Then a fully diamond armored skeleton joined the fight. I tried attacking the golem but it only breaks my sword! Even projectiles come back for me! I am going to die! Luckly my final shout crown triggered and launced me into the room above. "I nearly died!", I screamed with a severe sence of panic in my voice. I equipped my healing amulet and ate some golden carrots to quickly regenerate some health. I found a small room with a farm in it not so far away, where I setteled there to calm down a bit. 

Day probably 6 or 7:
Today I woke up early from a weaird charging sound. I peeped at the golem and saw It trying to fight something. Then it smashed down into the ground and created a blue shockwave. It came after me and out of panic I tried to block it with my shield. Luckly it worked, I didn't even take any damage! I felt way more confident now and further explored the dungeon. After a while I came across an enchanted sword stuck in a stone. I tried using it but it only came out a tiny bit. Some trial and error later I figured out that if I use it fast enough it lifts up more and eventually comes out! 

Day unknown:
I woke up and didn't see the golem downstairs so I tried exploring that section of the dungeon more. Then I noticed the sound of many blocks being broken. It broke through the wall and a purple beam burned me! Then I looked down the hole it created and there it was, the golem. I panicked and tried to block it with my shield. That didn't work so I wanted to block it of using some blocks. The only blocks I had on me where amethyst blocks and suprisingly it worked! Testing this with many different blocks, I found out that amethyst is the only block which reflects the lazer. 

Day or night unknown- of doom:
I felt very confident with the knowledge I just gained, So confident even thet I went even deeper into the dungeon. Then I entered a room which looked like a graveyard. A bad idea it was, it was full of mobs! Ewl! I ran away until I heard a weird teleporting sound. I looked behind me and there it was. The golem had just teleported behind me! I ran extremely fast, so fast even that I was shocked about the speed I was running with! I ran towards my safe room to which I've luckly made a very clear path using torches. I survived, or so I thought, I was in the safe room but I didn't have much food left and the golem was blocking the way out. I tried running past him but nearly died. Is this really my end? Starvation? After all of that.. What felt like a week later I'm officialy out of food, the golem won't move whatsoever and mining also doesn't work since my pickaxes all broke! I'm very hungry an tired of this dungeon, just let me out! haaaarrrarrararar...!! Tomorrow I will try again to run past the golem. If that won't work, this it probably the last you'll ever hear from me...
